Cellular Automaton Sonification

The grid runs a cellular automaton. Each tick: count neighbours, apply the rulebook, swap buffers. Sonification is deterministic: birth = note, column = beat, row = pitch. No random generators in the audio path.

1 · THE RULE

Each cell counts its 8 neighbours. The rulebook, written B3/S23, lists birth counts (a dead cell with those counts turns on) and survival counts (a live cell with those counts stays on):

n(x,y) = Σ alive(x+dx, y+dy) over the 8 neighbours next(x,y) = n ∈ B ? 1 : ( alive(x,y) && n ∈ S ? 1 : 0 )

2 · THE SCORE MAPPING

28 rows map to 8 pitch lanes; lanes hold scale degrees, not fixed frequencies. Selecting a scale retunes the whole board:

lane(r) = ⌊ r · 8 / rows ⌋ (top = highest degree) f(lane) = 220 Hz · 2^( degree(lane) / 12 ) (A-based, octaves folded) beat k = column k · (cols / 8) (left → right, wraps)

Per tick, each of the 8 voice columns checks for births; each birth sounds its lane's note, a 220–300 ms pluck. Simultaneous births sound together.

3 · THE ENVELOPE

gain(t) = e^( −t/τ ) after a 5 ms attack ramp τ ≈ 80 ms osc → lowpass (saw/square) → master (−14 dB headroom)

4 · WHY PENTATONIC

  • No semitone pairs: any set of simultaneous pentatonic degrees is consonant; arbitrary birth patterns stay musically stable.
  • Pattern-to-structure mapping: gliders repeat a pitch sequence per column-crossing; oscillators become repeating rhythm; still lifes hold a constant note or silence.
  • Rule changes texture: Seeds gives short percussive events; Life gives sparse sustained notes; Day & Night gives dense clusters.

5 · THE FINISH

Cells are stamped as burnished metal: newborn cells flash pale brass, age through the mid tones, and settle into dark oxide. The beat leaves a faint wash down its voice column; the sounding lane glows once and fades.

Build Record

Single self-contained sheet. No frameworks, no fonts, no samples. Synth: raw WebAudio; composer: byte-grid automaton.

ENGINEERING NOTES

  • Boards: two Uint8Arrays, double-buffered; neighbour counts per tick, edge wrapping (torus) or hard borders.
  • Audio per tick: births spawn oscillator + gain voices with exponential decay; collected after release. Master at −14 dB. AudioContext on first gesture.
  • Timing: fixed-step accumulator; tempo changes beat length, frame-rate independent.
  • Rendering: one canvas; age-toned batched rects; per-column birth flashes; 8 lane markers.
  • Presets: SOUP (30% Bernoulli), GLIDERS, PUDDLES, CLEAR. Pointer paints; painted births sound.
  • State sharing: rule + scale + voice + tempo + pattern RLE-encoded into the URL hash. Reload restores the exact state.
  • Reduced motion: autoplay disabled; static frame; STEP advances.

PERFORMANCE

One tick: 1,232 neighbour votes. One beat: at most 12 oscillator voices. A few hundred painted cells per frame; 60 fps on modest hardware.
