PLATE NOTES — SPECIMEN 02

CONTINUUM · SMOOTH-STATE CELLULAR PLATE · CATALOG 02/24

Each cell holds a real value, not a bit. Update rule: convolve the field with a ring kernel, pass the neighborhood average through a bell, add a fraction of the result back. Two equations, no exceptions.

Starting medium: speckle at the target density plus a set of dense nuclei disks. All structure on this plate condensed from that medium. Nothing is drawn.

RECIPES

  • GLIDERS — single band, mid radius. Self-propelled sparks cross the plate indefinitely.
  • ORBITA — two bands. Orb bodies with halos, elastic-looking soft collisions.
  • HYDRA — wide band, wide tolerance. Coral growth and retraction cycles.
  • WORMS — broad shallow band. Elongated forms, lateral undulation.
  • RESOT — two bands, dense. Churning mats, episodic starbursts.
  • SANDBOX — drag the K(r) profile; the plate re-derives from the new kernel.

OPERATING NOTES

  • Canvas drag pours saturated medium at the brush. P toggles eraser.
  • Mass readout: fraction of cells above 0.5. Living recipes hold 5-35% and keep changing; dead ones decay to 0.
  • Grid auto-scales to hold step time inside frame budget. RESEED after each downscale; the soup is not preserved.
  • Kernel truncated taps renormalize to sum 1.00. Unnormalized kernels cause systematic mass loss at every front.

METHOD — SPECIMEN 02

CONTINUUM UPDATE RULE, KERNEL, ENGINEERING

1 · UPDATE

U(t+1) = clip(U + Δt·(G(K∗U) − ½), 0, 1) G(u) = exp(−((u−μ)/σ)²)·2 − 1

K is the ring kernel, ∗ circular convolution, Δt the SPEED fader. μ sets the neighborhood average the matter seeks; σ its tolerance.

2 · KERNEL

K(r) = Σ bands w·exp(−((r/R − m)/s)²)

Each band: center m, width s, weight w. Kernel normalized to Σ=1 so the vote is a true average. Taps under 0.12% weight are dropped and the remainder renormalized — a 10% normalization error reads as uniform shrinkage of every structure.

3 · GEOMETRY

Torus. A margin ring of width R wraps both axes; edge cells read real content across the seam. Verified: single-spike response matches brute-force wrap convolution to 2×10⁻⁶.

4 · ENGINEERING

  • Two Float32 planes ping-pong; no in-place reads.
  • Flat typed arrays, precomputed tap offsets. 252-404 taps per cell at 60Hz on integrated graphics, ≈9-12 ms/step at 272×152.
  • Wrap computed in the margin fill, once per row — not per tap.
  • Clamp on write prevents ringing compounding to NaN.

5 · DEFAULT PLATE

GLIDERS: R13 μ0.150 σ0.030 Δt0.20 band m0.50 s0.15 w1.0

Recipe held alive across seeds. Wider tolerance widens survival; σ under 0.03 with this band is sterile.
