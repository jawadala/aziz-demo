PRINCIPLE

No triangles. The scene is one function answering a single question from any point in space: distance to the nearest surface. The function never overstates; each step is the largest safe stride. A ray from the camera through every pixel repeats the stride until it touches matter or escapes.

FORMULATION

Signed distance field f(p) → ℝ: negative inside matter, positive outside. From point p the largest step that cannot hit anything is exactly f(p).

repeat: t ← t + f(ro + rd·t) until f < ε (hit) or t > tmax (miss)

Primitives compose with min/max. The MELT control widens the polynomial bulge that fuses primitives:

smin(a,b,k): h = clamp(.5 + .5(b−a)/k, 0, 1) → mix(b,a,h) − k·h(1−h)
n = normalize( ∇f ) via central differences with step ε

Soft shadows are a second march toward the key light: the pixel dims by how narrowly the ray clears intervening matter. No shadow maps. Four studies. GARDEN — torus, sphere and cube melted over a gridded floor. METABALL — six drifting masses under a wide melt constant. BULB — distance estimator iterating z → z⁸ + c in spherical coordinates. GYROID — a sin·cos lattice, thickened and clipped by a sphere.

SERVICE DATA

  • Single file. No dependencies, no fonts, no images, no network calls.
  • Renderer: one fragment shader on a fullscreen triangle; the CPU uploads 13 uniforms per frame.
  • RES scales the framebuffer 0.35–1.0× device pixels; integrated GPUs hold 60 fps.
  • No WebGL: a software raymarcher takes over — same field functions, same loop, 96×72.
  • Orbit inertia after release; auto-orbit after 2.8 s idle, toggle A.
  • State lives in the URL hash. LINK copies it, camera included.
  • prefers-reduced-motion: single still frame; orbit redraws on demand.
