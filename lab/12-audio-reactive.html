PRINCIPLE

The cochlea already runs this math. Air pressure arrives as one wiggling number; hearing unmixes it into pitch bands. Same operation in arithmetic: take a window of raw samples, correlate it against pure sinusoids, and the magnitude spectrum that falls out drives the trace.

FORMULATION

Window of N samples against K discrete frequencies — the Discrete Fourier Transform:

X_k = Σ_n x_n · w_n · e^(−2πi·k·n/N) k = 0 … N/2

|X_k| is how much of frequency k lives in the window. The taper w_n softens the window edges; a hard cut smears energy across all bins. DAMP tames the analyzer's exponential decay on top:

band[i] ← max( band[i]·decay , sense · |X_k| ) (log-grouped bins)

Hearing is log-frequency: an octave is a doubling. The linear bins are grouped into ~96 geometric bands from ~40 Hz to ~12 kHz — each bar a constant-ratio slice. A bass note walks the left edge; a hi-hat flickers the right. The beat light is statistics: mean energy of the lowest bands against its own running mean. A spike means onset; onset drives the radial pulse and the particle kick.

The internal source is two detuned oscillators stepping through a chosen scale (ratios like 9/8, 5/4, 3/2) on a tempo clock. The trace always eats honest signal.

SERVICE DATA

  • Single file. No dependencies, no fonts, no images, no network calls.
  • Audio graph: source → analysis node (FFT 2048 → 1024 bins, ~23 Hz per bin at 48 kHz) → output. Mic path never routes to speakers — no feedback.
  • Mic needs a permit; denied or absent, the synth covers the stage. The trace shows real analyzed signal either way.
  • Four renderers over one shared 96-band spectrum: bars (strokes + peak caps), radial (mirror spokes + beat ring), waveform (dual-stroke line), particles (220 spring-orbiting motes banded to the spectrum).
  • EKG grid sits behind the trace with pointer parallax; the bass mean breathes the radial base.
  • Adaptive DPR cap and batched canvas paths hold 60 fps on integrated graphics.
  • State in the URL hash — LINK copies the exact rig. prefers-reduced-motion renders one static frame.
  • Everything audible is generated locally on demand. Nothing is recorded, stored, or transmitted.
