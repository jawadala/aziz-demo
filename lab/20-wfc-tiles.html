Doctrine — generation by observation

Every cell of the chart begins in superposition: a patch that could be road, field, water or wall. Generation is a chain of observations. Each step observes the cell with least freedom, forces it into one state, and the consequences ripple outward until the whole chart has settled.

1 · Tiles, sockets, adjacency

Each tile declares four sockets (E / S / W / N) from a small alphabet: land, road, water, trace, wall. Two tiles may sit side by side only when their facing sockets agree.

allowed(A → B, dir) ⇔ A.socket[dir] = B.socket[opposite(dir)] (+ declared exceptions: a bridge forgives water/land under its deck)

Rotations of one prototype are generated automatically, so one bend drawing yields all four bends.

2 · Entropy picks the next observation

H(c) = −Σ pᵢ log pᵢ , pᵢ = wᵢ / Σ wⱼ (weights of tiles still possible at cell c)

The cell with minimal entropy — plus a pinch of seeded noise as tie-breaker — is collapsed next. A weighted draw over the survivors chooses what it becomes.

3 · Constraint propagation

After every collapse a worklist propagates the blast radius: if every tile that could support a neighbour's candidate is gone, that candidate is pruned too, and the pruning cascades.

remove(c, t): for each neighbour n: for each t' in n: if no tile of c supports t' → remove(n, t'), enqueue n

The chart finishes when every cell holds exactly one survivor. A cell at zero is a contradiction: the run is declared and restarted from a jittered seed (the re-surveys counter).

4 · Seeded determinism

A text seed hashes into the random stream behind every tie-break and draw. Same seed + same plate + same scale ⇒ the identical chart, bit for bit. That is what makes seeds shareable.

Ledger — build record

One self-contained HTML file, zero dependencies. The solver, four hand-drawn tilesets and the renderer are written for this atlas in plain JavaScript + canvas 2D.

Engine

  • Solver — possibility bit-field per cell, weight-based Shannon-entropy selection, worklist propagation, contradiction counter with automatic re-seed.
  • Plates — Roads & Rivers (26 tiles incl. bridges with soft sockets), Circuit (pads, chips, vias), Terrain (forests, rivers, ridges), Rooms (wall / open / door grammar). Rotated variants are generated, not duplicated.
  • Renderer & camera — every tile painted once into a sprite sheet at device-pixel resolution, then laid on a projected table: rows compress toward the horizon, decided cells rise as lacquer pieces, undecided sockets drift gold dust. Margin-drag gimbal with inertia; idle sway otherwise.
  • Reduced motion — prefers-reduced-motion generates the whole chart instantly, no shimmer.

Interactions

  • Drag on the chart — collapses cells under the cursor (paint-to-observe).
  • Space halt · S single step · R re-survey · N new seed · T cycle plates · 1/2/3 scale.
  • Seed box — type any text, press Enter: deterministic chart. Copy URL puts tileset + scale + pace + seed in the hash.
  • Pace fader scales tiles-per-frame; One step advances exactly one observation.

Budget

Selection is a linear scan over a couple thousand cells; propagation touches only the blast radius. The largest scale finishes in well under a second. The ledger shows live decided / entropy / re-survey counts so the solver's progress can be read at a glance.
