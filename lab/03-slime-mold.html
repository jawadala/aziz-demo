PLATE NOTES — STUDY No. 7

PHYSARUM · AGENT/TRAIL FIELD · CATALOG 03/24

Forty thousand point agents. No direct communication. Each deposits trail, senses trail at three forward points, turns toward the strongest, moves. The trail diffuses and decays. Network forms.

The printed network is transport topology: thick veins carry high flux, thin branches probe. Cut a vein with the pointer and the colony reroutes. FEED DROP lays attractant; watch the mesh converge on it.

RECIPES

  • NETWORK — sensor 22°, distance 9. Canonical vein mesh.
  • VORTEX — sensor 60°, wide turn. Curling sheets, slow vortices.
  • FILAMENT — narrow angle, long sensor. Parallel hair-thin streaks.
  • MESH — short sensor, soft turn. Even lace.
  • PARALLEL — sensor 90°. Grid-like bedding.
  • SANDBOX — same engine, mild defaults; sliders are the experiment.

OPERATING NOTES

  • Trail mean readout: 0 = starved field, colonies die to uniform paper. RE-DERIVE keeps parameters inside the band that nets.
  • STATE→URL encodes recipe and faders. Reload resumes the same experiment.
  • Torus topology. Veins cross the sheet edge without seam.

METHOD — STUDY No. 7

STEERING, TRAIL DYNAMICS, ENGINEERING

1 · AGENT

sense FL, F, FR at (±SA, SD) ahead turn toward argmax by ≤ RA rad x ← x + v·(cosθ, sinθ) toroidal wrap trail[x,y] ← min(255, trail + D)

2 · TRAIL FIELD

diffuse: separable 3×3 box filter (two 1-D passes) decay: T ← T · (1 − DECAY)

Diffusion spreads attractant one cell per tick; decay (0.06-0.12) forgets. The two rates set vein width and lifetime together.

3 · ENGINEERING

  • Agents in flat Float32/Uint8Arrays; no objects in the hot loop.
  • Grid 256×144 nominal, independent of screen; render is one ImageData blit scaled to viewport.
  • All sensor reads wrap by modulo; no boundary special case.
  • Measured: 40k agents + field pass ≈ 6-9 ms/frame, integrated graphics, 60 fps.

4 · DEFAULT PLATE
