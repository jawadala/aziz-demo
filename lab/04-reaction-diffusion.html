PLATE NOTES — SPECIMEN 04

TWO REAGENTS · U/V COUPLED DIFFUSION · CATALOG 04/24

Two reagents on a lattice. U is fed continuously at rate F. V is produced where U meets V (u v² term) and drained at rate F+k. V diffuses at half the U rate. Every structure on this plate is that one reaction, iterated.

Feed rate F and kill rate k select the morphology. The pairs in the BATH list sit in distinct regimes of the (F, k) plane; the faders move you off them into transitional forms.

BATHS

  • CORAL f.0545 k.0620 — branching fronts, net growth.
  • MITOSIS f.0367 k.0649 — rounds that split, then split again.
  • WORMS f.0780 k.0610 — crawling loops, self-avoiding filaments.
  • SOLITON f.0300 k.0620 — stable dots, no growth.
  • LAVA f.0140 k.0540 — negative-feed regime: bubbles that close.
  • U-SKATE f.0620 k.0609 — skate profile, long-lived movers.

OPERATING NOTES

  • REAGENT V readout: fraction of lattice above 0.15. Living baths hold steady or oscillate; 0.0 means the bath burned out — RESEED.
  • Brush deposits V=1. P toggles drain-brush (V=0). DRAIN empties the plate.
  • STATE→URL encodes F, k, diffusion, dt. Reload resumes the bath.
  • Torus topology. Fronts cross the plate edge without reflection.

METHOD — SPECIMEN 04

REACTION LAW, LAPLACIAN, STABILITY

1 · REACTION

∂u/∂t = Du∇²u − u v² + F(1−u) ∂v/∂t = Dv∇²v + u v² − (F+k)v

Explicit Euler on a lattice, Δt = dt fader. Du 1.00, Dv 0.50 nominal. F refills U; the u v² term converts the pair; k removes V.

2 · LAPLACIAN

∇²x ≈ 0.05·(corners) + 0.20·(edges) − x

Nine-point stencil, isotropy to second order. Wrap indexing on both axes; no boundary layer.

3 · SEEDING

U initialized to 1 everywhere, V to 0. V seeded in a handful of small random blocks. Structure grows from the block edges; uniform V carpets die to the drain term.

4 · STABILITY ENVELOPE

  • dt above ≈ 1.5 with Du 1.0 violates the explicit limit; the lattice checkers, then NaNs. This is the honest edge: dt is capped below it.
  • F near 0 starves U; V everywhere decays to 0 regardless of k.
  • k above ≈ 0.073 with F below 0.03 is empty regime: no growth from any seed.

5 · DEFAULT PLATE

CORAL F 0.0545 k 0.0620 Du 1.00 Dv 0.50 dt 1.0 lattice 256×144 two substeps per frame

Two substeps keep fronts moving at 60 fps without raising dt past the stability edge.
