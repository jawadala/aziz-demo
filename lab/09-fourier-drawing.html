PRINCIPLE

A closed path is cut into 256 equal-arc samples, each read as a complex number. The direct DFT returns 256 rotating arms, sorted by magnitude. Rendered in perspective: long slow arms carry the silhouette, short fast arms carry corners, and the chain rises off the plane where the PITCH switch is engaged.

FORMULATION

Sample n as a complex value: z_n = x_n + i·y_n

c_k = (1/N) · Σ_n z_n · e^(−2πi·k·n/N) k = 0 … N−1
z(t) = Σ_k c_k · e^(+2πi·k·t) , t ∈ [0,1)

ARMS truncates the spectrum — a low-pass over geometry. 40 arms: caricature. 120: corners return last; fine detail lives in fast rotations. DEPTH sets perspective compression; zero is the elevation view. Drag orbits the assembly with inertia. Scroll sets DEPTH.

OPERATION

Drag on the field to ink a path; release resamples by arc length and transforms in place — 256 points in, 256 coefficients out, no fitting. LINK copies the URL hash; the hash carries the full state, camera included.

SERVICE DATA

  • Single file. No dependencies, no fonts, no images, no network calls.
  • Direct O(N²) DFT at N=256: ≈65,000 multiply-adds per transform, plain JavaScript.
  • Perspective projection, painter-sorted strokes, ground grid at y = −1.3.
  • Trail batched per frame; a 2,000-point trace holds 60 fps.
  • Pointer-capture inking, mouse and touch. Orbit inertia after release; auto-rotate when idle.
  • prefers-reduced-motion renders a complete static trace; orbit still redraws on demand.
