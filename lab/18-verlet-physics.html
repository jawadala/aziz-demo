Theory — position-based dynamics

Everything on the frame is a cloud of points and a mesh of distance constraints. No rigid bodies, no rotation matrices. Position-based dynamics keeps the system stable while cloth wrinkles, ropes pull taut and the ring membrane jiggles under load.

1 · Verlet integration

x' = x + (x − x_prev) · damping + a · Δt² x_prev = x

Velocity is implicit: the difference between the last two positions. One subtraction gives momentum, drag and gravity. The integrator does not blow up.

2 · Distance constraints

d = x₂ − x₁ , err = (|d| − rest) / |d| x₁ += d · err · ½ , x₂ −= d · err · ½

Each constraint pulls both endpoints toward its rest length. Sweeping the constraint list k times per frame (the stiffness fader) is relaxation: force propagates across the mesh the way Gauss–Seidel iterations converge.

3 · Tearing

if |x₂ − x₁| > tear · rest → member struck from the list

Over-stretched members fail. Grab a point and yank: stress spreads through the relaxation sweeps, neighbouring links pass the tear limit and the specimen rips along a fault line.

4 · Wind & collisions

a_wind = W · (0.6 + 0.4 sin(3.1y + 2.3t)) · (0.8 + 0.4 sin(1.7x − t))

A spatially modulated horizontal acceleration: gusts travel across the surface instead of pushing it uniformly. Points collide with the floor; penetration is projected out and tangential velocity is damped by nudging the implicit previous-position.

5 · The camera and the light

The weave hangs on a turntable: yaw and pitch with inertia, spring-righted when idle, so a specimen can be inspected from any angle mid-test. Threads shade by strain — platinum at rest, warm ember as a member approaches its limit — and the floor carries a faint reflection for depth. Loading physics never reads the camera; drag a node and the cloth, not the stage, moves.

Spec — build record

One self-contained HTML file: typed-array simulation, canvas 2D rendering, no libraries, no network. About 1,500 live constraints run at 60 fps on integrated graphics.

Engine

  • Points — flat Float32Array pairs (x, y) plus previous positions; pinned points flagged and skipped by integration.
  • Solver — per frame: integrate all points, then k relaxation sweeps over the constraint list, then floor projection. Failed members are tombstoned and compacted.
  • Specimens — cloth (40×24 grid pinned along the top every 7 columns), rope (64-link chain, heavy doubled tip), membrane (24-point ring, radial spokes to a centre mass).
  • Camera & light — pitch/yaw turntable with inertia and idle spring; strain shading platinum→ember; floor reflection. Applied at draw time only.
  • Reduced motion — prefers-reduced-motion renders a static posed frame; controls re-pose on demand.

Interactions

  • Drag any node — it follows the pointer and hauls the mesh with it. Yank past the tear limit to fail members.
  • Shear mode — C or the Shear button; drag the blade across members to cut each one crossed.
  • Space hold · R re-set · M next specimen · faders for gravity, wind, tear limit, stiffness.
  • Load tables T·1–T·4. State lives in the URL hash and survives reload.

Budget

Constraint data sits in flat arrays for cache locality. The readout stack reports live node / member / failure counts. DPR capped at 2 for mobile fill-rate.
